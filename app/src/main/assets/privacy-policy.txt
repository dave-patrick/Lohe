# Privacy Policy — Lohe

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Lohe is a podcast player from Atomic 22 Industries. Contact us about privacy or support at atomic22industries@gmail.com.

## Your library and preferences

Lohe stores subscriptions, feed addresses, episode metadata, listening positions and history, playlists, Up Next, downloaded audio, and preferences on your device. These are used to organize your library, resume playback, refresh subscribed feeds, and manage downloads. Lohe does not operate an account or library-sync server and does not automatically upload your listening history to the developer.

You do not need an account. Lohe does not include advertising, analytics, advertising identifiers, or an automatic crash-reporting service. It does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, or access to an inventory of your installed apps.

## Requests to podcast services

Searching for podcasts sends your search words to Apple's public iTunes catalog. Catalog lookups may also use a show's title to find a moved feed. Subscribing, refreshing, viewing artwork, streaming, or downloading connects directly to the relevant catalog, publisher, hosting service, or content delivery network. Scheduled updates can make these requests in the background when enabled.

These services receive the requested search words or resource address, your network IP address, and ordinary HTTP request information, including a Lohe app/version user agent. A publisher's server can learn which feed or episode you request and may keep its own logs. Their data practices are governed by their own privacy policies; the developer does not control these servers or their retention. Lohe does not send a copy of your full library or listening-history database with these requests.

Lohe uses HTTPS when the service address uses HTTPS and validates certificates through Android's system trust store. Some publisher and user-supplied feeds or audio addresses use HTTP. Those requests are not encrypted in transit and may be visible to your network provider or others on the network. Prefer HTTPS feeds, especially for private or authenticated subscriptions. Lohe cannot promise that every podcast connection is encrypted.

## Optional permissions and playback

Media playback can continue in the background using Android's media playback service and media controls. Optional floating controls use Display over other apps access to draw playback buttons over other apps. They do not read other apps' content or use an accessibility service. You can disable them in Settings → Floating player or revoke access in Android settings.

On Android 13 and later, Lohe offers optional notification access when you enable floating controls, so its ongoing notification can provide a Hide controls action. Denying this does not prevent ordinary audio playback. You can change notification access in Android settings. Local-file and backup imports and exports use Android's file picker and only the files or folders you choose.

The Google Play phone/tablet build does not advertise Android Auto or Automotive support. Other distributions may expose your media library and playback controls to a connected Android car media host. Android and the connected host handle their own platform services under their respective privacy policies.

## Diagnostics and information you choose to send

Local bug reports are optional in the release app. When enabled, Lohe keeps a limited set of diagnostic events and technical app/playback information in app-private storage; URLs are redacted and release reports do not capture Android logcat. Reports are not automatically uploaded. If you export a backup, share a report, or send a support email, you choose the recipient and what to send. Such files may include library, settings, or technical information. Review them before sharing.

If you contact us, your email address and the information you include are used to answer your request. Do not send passwords or private feed credentials. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; you can request deletion by email.

## Android backup and files you export

Depending on your device and Android backup settings, Android may back up or transfer app data, including your library and preferences, using your platform backup provider. Lohe excludes downloaded episode files and local diagnostic reports from its Android backup rules. The developer does not receive or control your Android-managed backup. Manage or delete those backups through your Android or backup-provider settings.

Files you export or keep in a folder chosen through Android's file picker can remain outside app-private storage. Their storage provider may have its own sync and retention behavior. Lohe does not delete those external copies when you uninstall it.

## Retention and deletion

Your app-private library and preferences remain until you change them, clear Lohe's app storage in Android settings, or uninstall Lohe. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes the app-private copy. Downloaded copies are also subject to the archive and retention settings you choose. Unsubscribing alone can preserve episode history; it is not a full data-erasure action.

Delete exported files, shared copies, and device/cloud backups separately through the relevant file manager, recipient, or backup provider. Removing a downloaded copy does not necessarily remove your original imported audio file. Lohe has no account to delete and no developer-hosted copy of your library that can be erased by an account-deletion request.

## Children and changes

Lohe is a general-purpose podcast player and is not designed specifically for children. Podcast content comes from third-party publishers and can include mature material. The store's content rating and a publisher's guidance should inform content choices.

We update this policy when Lohe's practices change. The date above identifies the current policy. For questions or requests, email atomic22industries@gmail.com.
