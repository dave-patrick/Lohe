## App name

Lohe — Podcast Player

## Short description

Discover podcasts, organize playlists, chapters & transcripts on your terms.

## Full description

Have you heard? Lohe keeps your podcasts, playlists, chapters, transcripts, and Up Next queue together so you can listen on your own terms.

Find your shows

Search the podcast catalog, subscribe to RSS feeds, and build a library of the conversations and stories you want to hear. Bring subscriptions and playlists with you using OPML or JSON import and export.

Keep listening

Resume episodes where you left off. Listen in the background with Android media controls, control playback from the home screen widget, or download episodes for offline listening. Your library, playback positions, bookmarks, and preferences stay on your device.

Podcasting 2.0 chapters, transcripts & bookmarks

Jump between episode chapters, search synced transcripts by keyword, and save timestamped bookmarks with personal notes.

Shape what comes next

Organize custom playlists and folders or use 7 smart playlists to find played, unplayed, in-progress, downloaded, and new episodes. Add episodes to Up Next, reorder your queue, and set individual podcasts to queue new episodes automatically.

Set your playback

Choose global or per-podcast playback speed, skip intervals, volume boost, skip silence, and a sleep timer with shake-to-extend.

Optional Lohe Pro (annual subscription)

Support independent development with an optional Lohe Pro subscription via Google Play:
- Draggable floating mini-player over other apps (uses optional Display over other apps access; no accessibility service; ordinary playback works without it)
- Extra Up Next indicator styles (Pill, Diamond, Glow) and custom HEX color picker
- Automatic Google Drive AppData cloud sync to your own Google Drive account

No Lohe account is required. Lohe includes no advertising or analytics SDK. Podcast publishers may include ads in their audio. Catalog search, podcast feeds, and optional Google Drive sync connect to third-party services; some feeds use unencrypted HTTP. See the privacy policy for details.
