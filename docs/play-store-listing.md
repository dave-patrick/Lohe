# Lohe — proposed phone/tablet listing

Owner reviews copy and content/audience choices before submission. No car claim is made for the first Play build.

## App name

Lohe — Podcast Player

## Short description

Discover podcasts, organize playlists, and keep listening where you left off.

## Full description

Have you heard? Lohe keeps your podcasts, playlists and next episodes together so you can listen on your own terms.

Find your shows

Search the podcast catalog, subscribe to feeds and build a library of the conversations and stories you want to hear. Bring subscriptions with you using OPML import and export.

Keep listening

Resume episodes where you left off. Listen in the background with Android media controls, or save episodes for offline listening. Your library, playback positions and preferences stay on your device, with Android-managed backups depending on your device settings.

Shape what comes next

Organize custom playlists or use smart lists to find played, unplayed, in-progress, downloaded and new episodes. Add episodes to Up Next, choose their order and set individual podcasts to queue new episodes automatically.

Set your playback

Choose playback speed, skip intervals, voice enhancement, volume boost, silence settings and a sleep timer. Optional floating playback buttons remain available over other apps when you grant Display over other apps access. They use no accessibility service, and ordinary playback works without them.

Manage downloads

Save episodes before you go and choose your archive and downloaded-copy retention settings. Open episode notes from playlists without starting playback.

No Lohe account is required. Lohe includes no advertising or analytics SDK. Podcast publishers may include ads in their audio. Catalog search and podcast requests connect to third-party services; some feeds use unencrypted HTTP. See the privacy policy for details.
