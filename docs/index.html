To hear.
To listen.
To take something to heart.

In Hawaiian, that is Lohe (lōhe). We built the app around the simple idea of listening on your own terms. Whether you are into gripping stories, honest conversations, the daily news, or a deep dive on a random obsession, Lohe lets you hear what you want, exactly how you want to hear it. It stays out of your way and remembers where you left off.

Your library,
your rhythm.

A focused podcast player with the controls to make every listen feel like yours.

Keep your place

Resume episodes where you stopped, with playback controls on the lock screen and in your car.

Shape Up Next

Reorder the queue, add episodes at the beginning or end, and let chosen shows add new episodes automatically.

Listen your way

Choose skip intervals, playback speed, voice enhancement, volume boost, silence controls, and sleep timer.

Make it yours

Organize custom playlists and use smart lists for played, in-progress, downloaded, and new episodes.

Take it offline

Download episodes and import or export your subscriptions with OPML.

Choose what stays

Completed episodes are marked played and leave playlists; choose when their downloaded copies are archived.

Start with a show
you love.

Install the current Android debug preview from the GitHub release. Android will ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager. Open Lohe, find a podcast, and add an episode to Up Next.

This is a prerelease debug build for testing, not a production-signed Play Store release. Android Auto supports media browsing and playback; Android Automotive dashboard support still needs verification on a head unit.
